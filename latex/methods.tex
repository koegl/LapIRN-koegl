\subsubsection{Preservation of PET quantification}
The scored MTV bias only constrains the \emph{net} lesion volume, so we
penalize it directly while supplying dense gradients through a Jacobian-based
surrogate. All terms are evaluated on the total transformation
$\varphi = \varphi_{\mathrm{aff}} \circ \varphi_{\theta}$, i.e.\ the affine
pre-registration composed with the predicted displacement, against the
original moving lesion mask $M$, since the affine itself is generally not
volume-preserving. First, we minimize the squared relative volume error
%
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{MTV}}
  = \left( \frac{\lvert M \circ \varphi \rvert - \lvert M \rvert}
                {\lvert M \rvert} \right)^{2},
  \label{eq:mtv}
\end{equation}
%
where $M \circ \varphi$ denotes the linearly resampled mask. This matches the
scored metric exactly, but its gradient is confined to the sub-voxel boundary
shell of the lesion. We therefore add a complementary term exploiting the
identity $\sum_{x \in W} \det J_{\varphi}(x) = \lvert M \rvert$, where
$W = \{\, x : M(\varphi(x)) = 1 \,\}$ is the fixed-frame (warped) lesion
region:
%
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{MTV\text{-}J}}
  = \left( \frac{1}{\lvert W \rvert} \sum_{x \in W}
    \det J_{\varphi}(x) - 1 \right)^{2}
  \label{eq:mtvjac}
\end{equation}
%
pins the same net volume ratio while providing gradients throughout the
lesion interior and, unlike a per-voxel constraint, still permits internal
redistribution of volume. Note that masking the determinant with the
moving-frame lesion, as is tempting, samples $\det J_{\varphi}$ at locations
unrelated to the lesion once the affine is composed in; empirically, this
decorrelated the surrogate from the scored MTV. Finally, the per-voxel
penalty
%
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{jac}}
  = \frac{1}{\lvert W \rvert} \sum_{x \in W}
    \left( \det J_{\varphi}(x) - 1 \right)^{2}
  \label{eq:jactum}
\end{equation}
%
acts as a stricter local regulariser, discouraging compensating compression
and expansion within the lesion. The same terms
(\ref{eq:mtv})--(\ref{eq:jactum}) are used during training and descended at
test time by our instance optimization, so the network is trained on the
objective it is later refined under.

# Paper editing checklist for the JMI extension

Re-reviewed 2026-10-25 after the corrected `align_corners` evaluations, using `main.tex` and its included manuscript, tables, figures, appendices, `../findings.md`, and the six supplied JMI examples (`024502`, `024503`, `034001`, `034002`, `044002`, `044003`). The current PDF is 31 single-spaced pages including references and appendices. The examples show presentation patterns, not current submission rules. Keep the `#NN` identifiers when discussing items. Reopened boxes mark earlier edits that need review against the new evidence.

**Recommended story.** Longitudinal PSMA PET/CT registration should improve anatomical correspondence without changing measurements of the *same* follow-up lesions. The corrected results support a bounded claim: loss-guided IO gains about three CT Dice points over network output without a detected increase in scan-level MTV/TLG change; IO without those losses increases both changes. This is an operating-point finding, conditional on predicted lesion masks and per-pair runtime. The remaining change is informative: predicted masks miss lesions, whole-scan losses permit opposing changes in individual lesions to cancel, and the soft direct MTV/TLG terms do not track the nearest-neighbor evaluation well. Per-bone rigidity is a separate anatomical result: rigidity alone improves particular PET endpoints, but adding it on top of PET-preservation losses gives no detected further PET benefit. Do not claim equivalence of preservation or demonstrated clinical benefit.

**JMI fit.** The supplied papers use a structured Purpose / Approach / Results / Conclusions abstract and lead from an imaging problem to a gap, method, quantitative evidence, practical meaning, and limitations. This draft has those components, but many ablations, tuning details, and appendix-scale results obscure the main decision. Give the clinical measurement problem and recommended operating point priority; use the ablations and rigidity analysis to explain its limits. Check live special-issue rules separately at submission (#29).

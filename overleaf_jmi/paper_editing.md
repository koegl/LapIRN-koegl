# Paper editing checklist for the JMI extension

Read from `main.tex` and its included files, against the six supplied JMI research papers (`024502`, `024503`, `034001`, `034002`, `044002`, `044003`). This is an editorial review of the present draft, not a claim that every feature of the examples is a journal requirement. The source compiles to a 38-page, double-spaced draft; the length includes references and the lists of figures and tables.

**Update after reading `../findings.md`.** Its checked entries describe findings already represented in the manuscript; its unchecked entries describe planned material or a story still to be fully realised. The boxes below are *editing actions*, so they stay unchecked until the corresponding manuscript change is done. In particular, the Q1 → Q2 → leave-one-out → baselines sequence is an agreed outline, not an accidental order to replace. The stencil-leakage measurements are confirmed, while the comparison of rigidity methods is still pending. The predicted-mask limitation is already supported by a test-set decomposition; the ground-truth-mask intervention remains a separate open test.

**What the examples suggest.** All six open with a structured Purpose / Approach / Results / Conclusions abstract, then move from an imaging problem to a specific gap, a testable approach, quantitative results, and a discussion of practical meaning and limitations. They vary in whether Methods and Experiments are separate and whether Discussion and Conclusion are combined. They include disclosures and a code/data availability statement. The useful model here is the *argument*, not a fixed section template. Your draft already has unusually good ingredients for that argument: a clinically motivated measurement problem, a clean training-versus-IO comparison, and an explanation for why preservation has a ceiling.

## Story and claims — settle these first

Keep that mechanism/result distinct from the Q1 PET-preservation finding. 5. ## Study design and technical interpretation

Keep challenge metric names consistent where they are needed for reproducibility.

The evaluation paragraph refers back to that definition; the per-lesion paragraph explains why whole-scan changes can hide lesion-level changes (`methods.tex:32–46`, `methods.tex:179–187`).

The rigidity-only TLG reduction with IO (11.9% to 10.1%) has Holm-adjusted $p=0.29$ and is no longer called significant in the manuscript or `findings.md`. Review before checking off this item.

13. [x] **#13 — Use a consistent folding description, including in `findings.md`.** The revised Results, Discussion, and findings notes now state that our variants stayed below the challenge's 0.005% NDV threshold on every test pair; several have nonzero NDV, including the illustrated pair. NiftyReg exceeded the threshold on 5/38 validation and 8/38 test pairs (21%), while its 1170 ppm test NDV is a mean over patients. Review the wording before checking off this item.

14. [x] **#14 — Check whether the comparison with baselines supports the intended takeaway.** The revised Methods states that all registrations use the same pairs and scorer; the CT-only baselines use no labels, while our network uses labelled training data and IO uses predicted labels. NiftyReg and ConvexAdam estimate their own affine transforms with different CT preprocessing; the previous claim that ConvexAdam received our affine was incorrect. Each baseline was tuned on validation with the same adapted composite ranking, among candidates within its own grid. Results frames the baselines as context for our accuracy–preservation trade-off, and Discussion cautions against choosing a method from Dice or PET change alone. Review these changes before checking off this item.

15. The Results and qualitative caption now interpret expansion, contraction, and the blue–white–red color scale under that convention. Review the wording and figure before checking off this item.

16. [ ] **#16 — Separate “rigid bones” from “preserved bone lesions.”** The rigidity penalty fits each fixed-scan bone, whereas the moving lesion mask is warped and may overlap bone only partly (`methods.tex:99–102`, `methods.tex:177–178`). 17.

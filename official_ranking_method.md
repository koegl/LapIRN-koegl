Test Phase Evaluation

The final leaderboard will be determined using a significance-based ranking procedure. This approach allows metrics with different units and numerical ranges to be combined without requiring arbitrary normalization.
Significance Scores

For each metric, methods will be compared pairwise using their subject-level results and a one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test.

The direction of improvement is defined as follows:

    Higher values are better for CT DSC and PET DSC.
    Lower values are better for CT HD95, PET HD95, absolute MTV bias, absolute TLG bias, and %NDV.

P-values will be adjusted for multiple pairwise comparisons within each metric using the Holm method. Method A will be considered to significantly outperform Method B when the adjusted one-sided p-value is less than 0.05 in the favorable direction.

For each metric, the significance score is calculated as:

significance score =
    (number of significantly outperformed methods + 1)
    / total number of methods

The significance score ranges from 1 / K to 1, where K is the total number of eligible methods. A higher score indicates that the method significantly outperforms a greater proportion of the competing methods. The addition of 1 ensures that all scores remain positive, which is required for geometric-mean aggregation.
%NDV Practical-Equivalence Threshold

To avoid penalizing negligible differences among nearly diffeomorphic methods, a practical-equivalence threshold of 0.005% will be applied to %NDV.

For each subject:

adjusted %NDV = max(%NDV - 0.005%, 0)

Subject-level %NDV values less than or equal to 0.005% are therefore treated as equivalent. Pairwise significance testing for deformation regularity will be performed using the adjusted %NDV values.

For an image size of 192 x 192 x 288, this threshold corresponds to approximately 531 voxels, or approximately 13.0 cm³ at a voxel spacing of 2.7344 x 2.7344 x 3.27 mm³.
Component Scores

The registration-accuracy score is the arithmetic mean of the significance scores for:

    CT DSC
    PET DSC
    CT HD95
    PET HD95

The PET-biomarker-preservation score is the arithmetic mean of the significance scores for:

    Absolute MTV bias
    Absolute TLG bias

The deformation-regularity score is the significance score for threshold-adjusted %NDV.
Final Score

The three component scores will be combined using a weighted geometric mean:

Final score = 100 x (
    registration-accuracy score ^ 0.4
  x PET-biomarker-preservation score ^ 0.4
  x deformation-regularity score ^ 0.2
)

Registration accuracy accounts for 40% of the final score because accurate alignment between baseline and follow-up PSMA PET/CT examinations is a primary objective of the challenge.

PET biomarker preservation accounts for 40% because preserving lesion volume and PET uptake measurements is equally important for the intended quantitative imaging applications.

Deformation regularity accounts for 20% and serves as a safeguard against methods that achieve high registration accuracy through substantial local folding. Methods with %NDV values within the practical-equivalence threshold will not be differentiated based on deformation regularity.

The weighted geometric mean rewards balanced performance across the challenge objectives. A method cannot fully compensate for poor PET biomarker preservation through strong registration accuracy alone, or vice versa.

Methods will be ranked according to their final scores, with higher scores indicating better overall performance. Methods with identical final scores will receive the same rank.
Ranking Stability

The official leaderboard will be calculated using the complete test cohort. Ranking stability will additionally be assessed using 1,000 subject-level bootstrap samples generated by sampling test subjects with replacement.

The complete scoring and ranking procedure will be repeated for each bootstrap sample. The resulting rank distributions and 95% rank intervals will be reported as a secondary analysis and will not alter the official leaderboard.

The same resampled subjects will be used for all methods within each bootstrap iteration to preserve the paired evaluation design.

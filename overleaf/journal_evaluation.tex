\subsection{Evaluation Endpoints and Statistical Analysis}
\label{sec:evaluation}
\subsubsection{Official PET preservation metrics.}
\label{sec:pet_evaluation_metrics}
For the official evaluation, PET is expressed in SUV and resampled using linear interpolation, while the binary lesion mask is resampled using nearest-neighbour interpolation. MTV is the physical volume of all lesion voxels. TLG is the sum of their PET intensities multiplied by the physical voxel volume. Both biomarkers are measured before and after registration.

Following the official challenge implementation, the error for either biomarker $Q$ is its absolute percentage change:
\begin{equation}
  E_Q(\%)=100\,
  \frac{|Q_{\mathrm{after}}-Q_{\mathrm{before}}|}
  {\max(Q_{\mathrm{before}},10^{-6})},
  \qquad Q\in\{\mathrm{MTV},\mathrm{TLG}\}.
  \label{eq:official_pet_error}
\end{equation}
Both official errors are aggregate scan-level measures: biomarker increases in some lesions and decreases in others can cancel before the absolute difference is taken, yielding a small aggregate error despite substantial individual lesion errors.
Because PET and the lesion mask are interpolated separately before multiplication, $E_{\mathrm{TLG}}$ captures both deformation-related changes and resampling effects at the lesion boundary.

\subsubsection{Metric implementation validation.}
Before model comparison, deterministic controls verify the complete metric implementation on a real PET/CT case.
An identity field must preserve MTV, TLG, Dice, HD95, and within-bone distances and produce no folding.
A whole-voxel translation, chosen to avoid field-of-view clipping, must preserve MTV, TLG, and within-bone distances, produce no folding, and recover the prescribed displacement in physical units.
A known uniform stretch must produce the expected relative within-bone distance change.
To characterize resampling alone, we additionally apply translations of $0.25$, $0.49$, $0.50$, $0.51$, and $0.75$ voxels while holding the underlying lesion and transformation geometry fixed.
For each shift, the official calculation above is compared with a control that first forms $I M$, linearly warps this product once, and sums the result over the whole image.
The $0.49$- and $0.51$-voxel conditions bracket the nearest-neighbour tie at exactly half a voxel.
These controls define a numerical resampling baseline and are not treated as registration-performance experiments.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Alignment:} per-bone and soft-tissue organ Dice and surface distance; near-bone landmarks only if independently available. Define aggregation over structures and handling of absent or truncated anatomy.
  \item \textbf{PET preservation:} retain the official aggregate MTV/TLG errors as primary endpoints. Additionally compute absolute MTV/TLG error for each source lesion, summarize lesion errors within each patient before cohort-level aggregation, and report a predeclared upper-tail statistic stratified by bone association and lesion size. Compare aggregate and per-lesion summaries to assess cancellation without changing the official endpoint.
  \item \textbf{Lesion measurement:} define lesions on the source scan and track them through the transformation. Retain one analysis row per registration pair and lesion, but give patients equal weight in cohort summaries so patients with many scans or lesions do not dominate. Predefine size bins, bone-association rules, minimum denominators, empty-mask handling, field-of-view exclusions, SUV units, and interpolation. Distinguish resampling effects from deformation effects using a specified control.
  \item \textbf{Bone shape:} measure distortion of sampled within-bone point-pair distances in physical coordinates, covering short and long distances. Predefine sampling, distance bins, and normalization; report BRE secondarily.
  \item \textbf{Deformation quality:} report NDV/folding inside bones, in an outside boundary band, and across the body. Specify masks, band width in physical units, denominators, and consistent units.
  \item \textbf{Boundary mechanism:} quantify the fraction of stencil samples outside bone and the fraction of bones/voxels retained after erosion. State how bones that disappear after erosion are handled.
  \item \textbf{Uncertainty:} report paired method effects with patient-level confidence intervals, accounting for multiple pairs and lesions per patient. A candidate is patient-cluster bootstrap resampling that retains all within-patient observations and method pairing.
\end{itemize}

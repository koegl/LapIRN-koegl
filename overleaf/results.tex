\subsection{Metric Implementation Validation}
On PSMAReg subject 0006 at time point 02, the identity control produced zero MTV and TLG error, zero within-bone distance distortion, zero folding, Dice of one, and HD95 of zero.
A non-clipping whole-voxel translation likewise preserved MTV, TLG, and within-bone distances exactly, produced no folding, and recovered the prescribed $26.91$~mm displacement.
For subvoxel translations of $0.25$, $0.49$, $0.50$, $0.51$, and $0.75$ voxels, the official absolute TLG errors were $1.50\%$, $2.94\%$, $2.95\%$, $2.76\%$, and $1.41\%$, respectively, despite the transformation being a pure translation.
In contrast, linearly warping the pre-multiplied PET--mask product changed its global sum by at most $1.4\times10^{-4}\%$.
MTV was preserved on either side of the nearest-neighbour tie ($0.49$ and $0.51$ voxels); at exactly $0.50$ voxels, tie-dependent nearest-neighbour assignment changed MTV by $0.16\%$.
These controls confirm the implementation and quantify a case-specific interpolation floor; they do not measure registration accuracy or biological change.

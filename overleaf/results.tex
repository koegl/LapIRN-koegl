\section{Results}

\subsection{Evaluation Protocol}
\label{sec:evaluation}

We report three configurations of our method.
\emph{Ours (container)} is the submitted method of Sec.~\ref{sec:container}; it is the configuration evaluated against the baselines because it matches the hidden-test submission.
\emph{Ours (validation)} uses the unconstrained pipeline of Sec.~\ref{sec:pipeline}, while \emph{ours (no IO)} removes instance optimization to isolate its contribution.
Significance markers are shown only for comparisons between \emph{ours (container)} and the baselines.

Table~\ref{tab:baselines} is evaluated on the 20 official challenge validation cases using our surrogate labels and local evaluator.
These results are therefore separate from the official Codabench validation leaderboard, which uses the organizers' scoring setup.

\subsection{Comparison against Baselines}

We swept the loss weights and the instance-optimization settings and selected the one reported below with the challenge ranking of Sec.~\ref{sec:model_selection}, applied to the surrogate protocol above.
Table~\ref{tab:baselines} places the three configurations against the baselines.
The container configuration improves DSC from $73.3\%$ (NiftyReg, the strongest baseline) to $81.0\%$ and reduces HD95 from $7.09$ to $6.17$\,mm, while also reducing the MTV and TLG errors and the non-diffeomorphic volume from $1525$ to $864$\,ppm.

\subsection{Leaderboard Performance}
These leaderboard numbers are reported separately because they were computed by the official evaluation server rather than by our surrogate-label protocol.
On the validation leaderboard, our method (\emph{Ours (validation)}) achieved a Dice score of $0.789 \pm 0.0381$, HD95 of $5.99 \pm 2.39$\,mm, MTV error of $1.56 \pm 1.22\%$, TLG error of $4.18 \pm 2.49\%$, and NDV of $0.0041 \pm 0.0024\%$.
